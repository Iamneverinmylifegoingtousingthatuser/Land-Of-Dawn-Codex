ABOUT MLBB

WHAT IS MOBILE LEGENDS?

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game where two teams compete against each other. Players choose unique heroes with different abilities and roles to destroy the enemy's base.

GAME OBJECTIVE

The main objective is to work together with your teammates, defeat enemies, destroy turrets, and ultimately destroy the enemy team's base to achieve victory.

HERO ROLES

TANK

Durable heroes who protect teammates and absorb damage.

FIGHTER

Heroes with balanced damage and durability.

ASSASSIN

Heroes specializing in eliminating important enemy targets.

MAGE

Heroes who deal magic damage and provide powerful abilities.

MARKSMAN

Heroes who deal consistent damage, especially during the late game.

SUPPORT

Heroes who assist teammates through healing, shielding, and crowd control.
