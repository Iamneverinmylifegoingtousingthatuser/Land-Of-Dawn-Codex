WHO ARE WE?

Welcome to our Mobile Legends: Bang Bang fan website! This website was made by Liam Aki Am Calinog and Paul Matthew G. Rance, created for players who want to explore heroes, learn about their abilities, and improve their knowledge of the game.

Our goal is to provide a simple and accessible platform where MLBB players can find useful information about different heroes, their skills, roles, recommended builds, and gameplay tips.
